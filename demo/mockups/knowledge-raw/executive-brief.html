The Houthis are striking Saudi economic targets, and Riyadh expects the war to last into 2027

Established · strikes confirmed by 5 independent outlets
What happened
  • Missiles damaged Riyadh's tallest building and a tower owned by the Public Investment Fund on 19 Nov.

The energy shock from the Iran war looks structural, not temporary

Established · IEA assessment reported by 3 independent outlets
What happened
  • The International Energy Agency called the crisis the largest threat to energy security in history.
  • Its chief sees no credible pathway back to pre-war normality in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Iran began targeting vessels on the southern route through Omani waters.
Why it matters

Energy and freight costs tied to the Gulf should be planned as a lasting premium, not a spike that reverses. The detour through Omani waters is no longer a safe alternative.

What to watch
  • Further attacks on ships in Hormuz and on the Omani route.
  • Progress towards a deal to end the Iran war.

UK inflation is rising again on energy, and the Bank of England has started raising rates

Reported · 2 independent outlets (Guardian, BBC)
What happened
  • Inflation rose to 3.6% in October, driven by petrol and record diesel prices.
  • The Bank of England raised rates for the first time in three years earlier this month.
  • Typical household energy bills are forecast to rise 26%, to £2,166 a year, from January.
Why it matters

UK operating costs and borrowing costs move up together, and the Bank expects inflation to keep rising until the middle of next year.

What to watch
  • The January energy price cap.
  • The Bank of England's next rate decision.
