One question for you. The “Wrong” and “Missed” lines in these two cards were checked by hand. The page cannot write them by itself. On live articles it can show Got, Unsure and Not built automatically. It can also warn about the few errors it can spot itself: a number inside a name like G20, a link with no article, a headline that does not match its text. Is that the right split?
