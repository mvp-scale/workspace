What this is. Real news articles arrive from the news reader. Each one goes through the same chain of steps, in order. For every article this page keeps a record of what each step did, including steps that found nothing, failed, or are not built yet. Nothing is picked or hidden, and no article is scored against labels.
How to use it: read 1 to see whether it is working, 2 for the steps, 3 for what the data says so far, then press the button in 4 to watch one article go through every step. Everything below that is detail you can open when you want it.
