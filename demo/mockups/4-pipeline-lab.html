What is real here. The transcript (Apollo 13) is real. Everything else is scripted: scores are generated, not produced by a model. The lag figures are the per-checkpoint times measured earlier on this machine for a 9-question checkpoint (JEV113 about 0.3 s, KEV4B about 1.1 s, SEMIF4 about 1.8 s). The window study on 200 real dialogues found the last 5 words or one sentence weakest and about 20 to 40 words (or 2 to 3 turns) best. JEV113 is the hosted model: in a real build it would run only after you confirm the cost.
