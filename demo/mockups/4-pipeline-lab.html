What is real. The transcript is the real Apollo 13 recording (public domain); the tags on it and every score here are scripted. The lag figures are the per-checkpoint times measured earlier on this machine for a 9-question checkpoint (JEV113 about 0.3 s, KEV4B about 1.1 s, SEMIF4 about 1.8 s). In the window study over 200 real dialogues, the last 5 words or one sentence were weakest, and about 20 to 40 words (or 2 to 3 turns) were best. The heavy black line is what the user sees (held for 30 s); the coloured lines are each model's raw answer for the shaded window, which drops as soon as the phrase leaves it. JEV113 is the hosted model: a real build would run it only after you confirm the cost.
