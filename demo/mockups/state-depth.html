State depth test: does adding to the state, the instructions or the criteria help the CPU classifier?

One frozen encoder turns the text into a vector; one small trained head per question (logistic regression, the CPU engine's own settings) turns that vector into yes/no. We change only what text goes into the encoder, train on 12 to 248 examples, and score on the same 200 test sentences from chats the training never saw. Labels are human (Persuasion for Good, annotators saw the whole chat). Nothing on this page calls a model; every number is read from data/state-depth/grid.json, produced by probes/state_depth.py grid.

Score: balanced accuracy = the average of "share of real yes caught" and "share of real no kept"; chance is 50%. Baseline: the sentence alone, same encoder. Pass: a cell beats the baseline at 248 by more than its 95% range, or reaches the baseline's 248 score with 100 examples or fewer.
