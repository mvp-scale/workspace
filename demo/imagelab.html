Image lab

Can a vision model do real business tasks on images? Every model below answers the same images, each with a known answer, and is scored the same way as the Scenario lab.

Caveat: each task has only 25 images, so the interval is wide (about plus or minus 15 points at 80%). Answers come from a dataset's own annotations or from a generator that drew the image, never from a person or model looking at it. Several sources are licensed for local use only, so these images stay on this machine. Read the interval, not just the point value.
