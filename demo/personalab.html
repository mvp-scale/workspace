Persona lab

Mock-up. Generate seeded personas, read them into numbers, give an idea, and see which part of the audience lines up with it. Runs on the local Winnow model.

This is a model-imagined audience, not a measured one. The personas are generated by our own rules and the answers come from the model. Offline checks (probes/persona/, BRIDGE.md) show it responds to the right levers; it has not been checked against real people.
