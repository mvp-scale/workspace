What a typed decision model is

A typed decision model takes a piece of state and a typed question with a fixed set of options, and returns a probability for every option. It never writes prose: the answer is the distribution itself, so software can act on it, threshold it, or check how well calibrated it is.

Three question types. In the TypeSafe /v1/systemone format a question is one of choice (pick one of several options), score (the options are points on a scale) or noul (a yes or no question, answered as the probability of yes). Every model below is called with the same three types.
