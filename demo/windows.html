Text windows

Run a battery of small typed detectors over a text a sentence at a time, then over sentence-plus-context windows and whole paragraphs, and stack the results. The windows, rolling scores and thresholds are composed here in page code; each model call is one independent typed question set.

Technique signals, not verdicts. A high score means the wording resembles a technique the detector was validated on. It says nothing about anyone's intent or honesty, and rhetorical appeals to fear, urgency or emotion can be entirely legitimate in a real speech. The accuracy shown beside each detector was measured on its own published labelled set, small models were near chance on some sets, and it does not transfer automatically to other kinds of text.
