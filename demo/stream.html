Call monitor

Replay a real transcript word by word and watch about a dozen perspectives update as it unfolds, to see whether something happened mid-call and which perspectives noticed. Every reading is the same text put through a small typed question; the windows, streaming and gates are composed here in page code.

Technique signals, not verdicts. Models return probabilities, not written reasons, so the only "why" this page can give is the evidence: the exact trailing window that was scored at that moment and how the score moved. A high reading means the wording resembles what a detector was validated on, not that anyone intended anything. Each detector's measured accuracy is shown beside it; some are weak or near chance for small models, and the threat set has label noise. Documented events come from cited sources, not from us. The window study's labels belong to whole dialogues and are not localised to any turn.
