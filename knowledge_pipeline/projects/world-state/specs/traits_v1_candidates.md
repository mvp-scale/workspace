# Revised trait wordings, version 1 (T41 step 1)
New versions of the two traits closest to GO (x013 and x031 pass reliability and the in-the-text check; their wording was not established, MM3-0610 and MM3-0611). Frozen v0 wording stays in specs/traits_v0.json. Each question is asked as yes or no about a short news item (headline plus teaser); the boundary line is part of the definition and is NOT shown to the raters unless stated.

## t_x013v1 (revision of t_x013)
Question: Does the story say that the price or charge for something households commonly pay for, such as food, fuel, housing or bills, has gone up?
Boundary rules: Counts: a reported rise that has already happened or is in effect. Does not count: a forecast or warning of a future rise, a cost rise that affects only companies or governments, or a fall in a price.

## t_x031v1 (revision of t_x031)
Question: Does the story report pollution, damage to wildlife or habitats, or a hazard to land, water or air, whether it has happened or is being warned about?
Boundary rules: Counts: a reported or warned-about environmental harm named in the item. Does not count: a purely economic or political risk with no environmental harm named, or an environmental benefit.
