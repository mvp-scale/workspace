# manipulation_windows (window study, not a probe set)
- File: manipulation_windows.json ({"dialogues":[{id, source_id, turns:[{speaker,text}], n_turns, manipulative, technique[], vulnerability[], label_scope}], ...metadata}).
- Source: MentalManip (Wang, Yang, Hassanpour, Vosoughi; ACL 2024), https://github.com/audreycs/MentalManip, mentalmanip_dataset/mentalmanip_con.csv (consensus). Licence CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial). Retrieved 2026-09-21. Citation: Wang et al. 2024, "MentalManip: A Dataset For Fine-grained Analysis of Mental Manipulation in Conversations", ACL 2024, pp. 3747-3764.
- LABEL SCOPE: the manipulative label, technique and vulnerability annotations apply to the WHOLE DIALOGUE. They are NOT localised to a turn, so a window study cannot assume the manipulation is present in any particular trailing window; a non-manipulative-looking window of a positive dialogue is still labelled positive.
- Parsing: the CSV stores each dialogue as one string with "PersonN:" prefixes. A turn starts at each line beginning with `Person<digits>:`; unprefixed continuation lines are appended to the current turn with one space; whitespace normalised. 5 dialogues could not be parsed (no leading speaker prefix or an empty turn) and were dropped. Speaker names are kept as in the source (Person1/Person2).
- Selection (seed 20260921, build_manipulation_windows.py): exclude 80 source_ids in manipulation_dialogue.jsonl; keep 3-12 turns and <=1400 chars (source text, no truncation); manipulative rows require a non-empty consensus technique (as in the existing set). Excluded counts: 687 turns out of range, 190 manipulative with no technique, 24 too long, 5 unparseable. Pools: 1299 manipulative, 630 not. Sampled 100 + 100 = 200, ids "mmwin-<ID>", verified disjoint from the existing set. Turn counts range 3-12 (mostly 3-5). Techniques: 80 dialogues have one, 18 two, 2 three; 32 have a vulnerability annotation (non-manipulative ones have none).
- Caveats: dialogues are Cornell Movie-Dialogs excerpts; annotation is subjective; the 3-12 turn filter skews to short dialogues, so trailing windows of 1-2 turns often cover most of the dialogue.
- Contamination: public since 2024; models may have seen the data.
