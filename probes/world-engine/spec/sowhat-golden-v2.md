# So what v2: golden cases (draft, 2026-10-06)

These are the unit tests for the v2 composer (`so-what-v2-design.md`, `so-what-v2-contract.md`). The machine-readable file is `sowhat-golden-v2.json`: 33 cases, each with its inputs, the fixed seed, the numbers the rules read, and the expected output. A composer passes when it reproduces, for every case, `headline`, `line2`, `hook_family`, `hook`, `body`, `register` and `anchor` (kind, raw value, shown value, where).

**Where the inputs come from.**
- Cases on fixtures (`F01`..`F22`): `spec/sowhat-fixtures/<n>.json` plus the classifier answers stored in v1's `sowhat-golden.json` (`input.classifier`), derived into a story exactly as v1's `deriveStory`.
- Cases on the 120-story diversity run (`s001`..`s120`): the engine responses live in a session scratchpad, so the JSON embeds what the composer reads: `impact.story`, the readings, rank, significance, and `rows` plus `places` trimmed to the rows a composer reads at that focus (world, the 8 audiences, countries with population share 0.5% or more, the entry and focus country and their regions). The full responses give the same outputs (checked).
- The two new backend fields are **test inputs, not engine output yet**, and are marked as such: `input.if_true` (linear estimate: places / weight) and `input.significance_rows` (the Size formula with regions standing in for per-person reach). When the engine sends the real fields, re-derive the cases that use them (every IF case, every local or non-world case); the rules do not change.

**How the expected outputs were made.** By applying the rules in the design spec exactly, through a reference implementation in the session scratchpad (Python, not repo code) so that every seeded pick is computed rather than guessed, then checked by hand: the eight worked derivations below were redone step by step from the numbers and hash values, and every case was read for grammar, honesty and the compatibility rules. `expect.picks` lists every seeded choice as index of candidates (it includes slots that were computed and then replaced by the anchor or dropped by the budget, for example a theme verb under an anchor body).

**Coverage.** Families: IF 7 (forecast 3, opinion 1, announced at reduced weight 2, pending 1), WHO_LOCAL 7, WHO_MOST 1, TURN 2 (one builds, one turn_shift), SCALE 3, STAKE 1, CONTRAST 2, TRIGGER 3, and every quiet case: QUIET_SMALL 2, QUIET_FORECAST 1, QUIET_OPINION 1, QUIET_ELSEWHERE 1, QUIET_VAGUE 1, NOTHING 1. Registers: stark 4, plain 18, hushed 4, none (quiet) 7. Anchors: 15 (A1 10, A4 if-true 4, A3 multiple 1), 6 in the headline and 9 in line 2 (7 moved there by the 22-word budget, recorded in `shrink`; G05 and G07 by rule: A3 never sits in a WHO body, and a turn-shift hook takes only the two-horizon bodies); 18 cases have no number. Foci: world 28, country 4, audience 1. Story kinds asked for: forecast (G14, G18, G19, G20), opinion (G15, G21), vague (G28), local (G04, G13, G23 to G27, G33), worldwide (G03, G11, G31), mixed (G08, G22, G26), tiny effect (G29, G30, G10), large effect (G03, G06, G11, G31), and the same frame three times (G23 to G25). WHO_OPP and QUIET_NOTABLE have no case: no story in either set produced them at these foci.

**Same frame, three statements (G23 to G25, all squeeze, all local, same family).** v1 gave "Only Brazil feels this squeeze: ...", "Japan takes this squeeze almost alone: ...", "Spain takes this squeeze almost alone: ...", and every pair shared a five-word run ("so people there cut back", "takes this squeeze almost alone", "keep savings in cash little changes elsewhere"). v2 gives three different hooks, three different bodies and two different anchor phrasings; one pair (G23, G25) still shares one five-word run, the anchor unit "in every 100 people in", by design (design section 14).

**Honest about weak numbers.** G10 (malaria vaccine) says people "relax their guard and take more trips": faithful to the guessed decision weights, not a good so-what. G16 (a baby giraffe) is a no-impact story whose topic the classifier placed in "pollution and nature"; the sentence says what the classifier said. G26 (Egypt) names government budgets, not fuel prices, as the other side, because the engine read fiscal strain more strongly. G18, G28, G29 and G32 have nothing to say beyond what the story is about, and say that.

## Worked derivations (by hand)

Notation: `h(slot) = djb2(seed + "|" + slot)`; the pick is `h mod n` over the candidates in the stated order.
