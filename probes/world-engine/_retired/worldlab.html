World lab

Put something in front of an audience and see who backs it, why, and what would move them. 50,000 simulated people grouped by how they think and live, plain arithmetic, no AI agents.

Demo, not a survey. The people are simulated, and how hard the world pushes on them is our assumption. The starting world comes from public indices, cross-checked against this week's news. Read every number as “what our model says”. “Support” here means the likelihood of taking the offer up.
