25 separate UAV photos from `Arvin26/cattle-detection`. The count is the number of labelled rectangles in that photo's LabelMe JSON (standing and laying). Bins are 1, 2-4, 5-12, and 13 or more (7, 6, 6, 6). Photos are spread across dates and altitudes, and one full frame is used per item. Licence is not stated in the dataset note, so this set is local use only.
